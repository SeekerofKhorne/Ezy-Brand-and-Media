      How import works

      A PDF downloaded from Canva or another design tool is taken apart: each text block becomes an editable text layer, each image and shape its own movable layer. Use the standard PDF download, not a flattened one. Then restyle it with the brand at the top of the page, or edit layer by layer.

      Quick start

      1. Describe the brand. On the Brand page, type a few words such as “calm, trustworthy”. The fingerprint shows the personality and feeling those words ask for.
      2. Build the kit. Choose colors, a type pairing and a graphic. The ones that fit your words come first, each with the reason it matched. Save palettes to the brand, then choose Save brand.
      3. Make posts. On the Media page, pick a platform, a format and a layout, replace the sample text, and save the post to the brand or export it.
      How much control do you want?

      You can switch at any time at the top of the page. Nothing is lost when you switch.

      Work is organized as brands. Each brand keeps its colors, named palettes, type pairings, logo and posts. Switch brands at the top of the page; manage, share and export them under Brands and posts. Brand changes are kept only when you choose Save brand.
