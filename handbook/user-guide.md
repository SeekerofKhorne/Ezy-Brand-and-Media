# User guide

## The two pages

**Brand** builds the brand kit, in two steps:

1. **Describe the brand.** Type a few words. Suggestions appear as you type (arrow keys to move, Enter to add); a half-typed word takes the top suggestion, and a word the app does not know is added and reported, not guessed. The **fingerprint** shows what the words ask for on eight axes: sincerity, excitement, competence, sophistication and ruggedness (none to strong), and pleasure, arousal and dominance (low to high, with the dashed line as neutral). Anything you asked for that the axes cannot show is named under the chart, with the reason: styles and topics are not feelings or personality traits, and four emotions (Compassion, Love, Amusement, Admiration) have no pleasure-arousal-dominance position on file yet. **Browse all keywords by group** opens the full index. Choose **Use these keywords** when done.
2. **Build the kit.** Pick colors, a type pairing and a graphic; the ones that fit your words come first. Each lists why it matched and carries a letter: **R** a published study measured the link, **S** a standard or platform requirement, **C** common practice nobody tested. Many everyday words and slang terms come from a reviewed list suggested by a language model; they carry no letter, and hovering the word says so. The small fingerprint compares your keywords (solid) with what your current picks signal (dashed). The preview shows a sample post as a square post, a story or a banner. **Identity** holds the brand name, handle and logo.

Brand changes are kept only when you choose **Save brand**. The Brand tab shows a dot while there are unsaved changes, and switching brands asks first.

**Media** makes posts. It has two modes, switched beside the page tabs: **Templates** and the **Layer editor**. In Templates, the rail on the left opens one panel at a time:

- **Layouts**: platform, format and layout. Pink hatching on the preview marks areas the platform covers or crops; it is never exported.
- **Text**: the words on the post. **Photo**: a photo for photo layouts.
- **Brand**: switches the post between the brand's colors and its saved palettes. Type and graphic come from the brand; change them on the Brand page.
- **Adjust** (Advanced): text size, photo framing, brand mark. **Posts**: this brand's saved posts.

Above the preview, **Default / Brand fill / Reversed** choose which brand color fills the post, and **Shuffle** tries another layout, color arrangement or saved brand palette. It never changes the brand's type or graphic. Below it, name the post, choose a **Folder**, and **Save post**, or **Export PNG**. A saved post keeps its own look when you later change the brand; a new post takes the brand's.

**Draft check**, under the preview, says in one line whether text contrast passes WCAG 2.2 and how many notes there are. Open it for the strongest associations of the current colors, type and graphic, and the cautions.

## Settings

The gear at the top right opens **Settings**:

- **Theme**: Neon Noir (the default), Clean Light, Clean Dark or Graphite. The preview area stays neutral gray in every theme, so the app's colors never shift how a brand's colors look.
- **How much control to show**: Simple or Advanced, each described in full.
- **Help**: the quick start, and the sources and method behind every association.

## Colors

- **Premade palettes** are five colors with roles: ground, panel, primary, accent, text.
- **Saved to this brand**, at the top of the Colors tab, lists the brand's own palettes; select one to switch to it, or save the current colors as a new one.
- **Build your own** lets you pick a role and choose its color: from single colors linked to your keywords, from partners that suit your primary and match a keyword, by typing a hex code, or with the color picker. Save the result as a named palette; it is kept with the brand and listed first.

## Library

A brand keeps its colors, named palettes, type pairings, logo and posts. Switch brands at the top of the page. In the **Library** you can create, rename, duplicate and delete brands, reopen or export any post, and:

- **Folders**: each brand's posts can be sorted into folders, one level deep. Choose a folder when you save a post, or move it later from its card. Deleting a folder keeps its posts, with no folder.
- **Copy to brand**: copies a post into another brand, restyled in that brand's colors, type, graphic, name and handle. The original is unchanged. Layer layouts are copied as they are.

- **Export brand kit**: a ZIP with a guidelines PDF, logo or wordmark, palette files (hex list, CSS, design tokens, Adobe swatch file) and the font files. Unzip it before giving the pieces to another tool.
- **Share brand as a file / Share (a post)**: one file that holds the brand or post and its images. The other person chooses **Open a backup or shared file** in their copy.
- **Back up everything**: one file with all brands, posts and images. Open it the same way to restore.

Work is stored in your browser on your device. Back up before clearing browser data or changing computers.

## Layer editor

Use it when a template is not enough.

- **Import a PDF or image.** A PDF from Canva or another design tool is taken apart: each text block becomes an editable text layer, each image and shape its own layer. Use the standard PDF download, not a flattened one. Typefaces that are not bundled are replaced and listed in the notes.
- **Start a blank canvas** at any platform size, or type a custom size.
- **Edit.** Select a layer on the canvas or in the list. Drag to move, pull a handle to resize, arrow keys nudge (Shift for larger steps), Delete removes, Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes, Ctrl/Cmd+D duplicates.
- **Apply brand colors / Apply brand type** restyles the layout with the open brand.

## Advanced mode

Switch in **Settings**. Advanced adds:

- **Build a pairing** (Brand page, Type tab): start from any ready-made pairing, then choose the headline and supporting typefaces and weights yourself. Associations follow the headline face.
- **Fine-tune hex values and graphic** (Brand page): all five hex values at once, graphic strength, rearrange the graphic.
- **Text size, photo and brand mark**: headline and supporting text size, headline capitals, which part of a photo stays in view, tint strength, the shade behind text on photo layouts, and whether the brand mark and handle show. These are saved with the post.
- **Edit as layers**: opens a copy of the template post in the Layer editor. Text, buttons, bars and the logo become separate layers; the ground, photo and graphic become one locked background layer. The original post is unchanged.
- **Export JPG** and **Export all sizes (ZIP)**: one PNG for every format of the current platform. A format keeps the current layout where it offers it, otherwise it uses its first layout, so check each image.
- **Shuffle changes**: choose whether Shuffle tries another layout, color arrangement or saved brand palette.

Posts made in Advanced open normally in Simple, with a note and a reset link if they use Advanced settings.

## Reading the evidence honestly

The associations are averages from studies of colors, typefaces and shapes shown one at a time. They are a way to narrow options and to explain a choice, not a prediction of how a post will perform. Brightness and saturation move feeling more than hue does, and fit with the product matters more than any single color. **Sources and method** in the app lists every source and its limits.
